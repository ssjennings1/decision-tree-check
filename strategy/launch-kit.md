## 5. LinkedIn document #1 (week +1): "Ten things that landed on my desk"

Carousel, 11 slides, square. Cream background, charcoal text, gold accents. Personal, not corporate.

1. **Ten things that landed on an owner's desk last week.** (Every one of them had a right answer. None of them needed her.)
2. A customer called to ask when the order would be ready.
3. Two guys on the crew wanted to swap days.
4. A tool broke. A few hundred bucks.
5. The supplier said the delivery would be a day late.
6. Somebody asked for Friday off.
7. A regular asked if we could squeeze in a rush job.
8. Somebody caught a quality problem before it went out.
9. The crew needed to know which job to start first.
10. A software renewal came due.
11. **Where did yours land?** Two minutes, no grade. syncovatellc.com/desk

Post text: "I asked an owner to write down everything that reached her desk in one week. This was Tuesday. Not one of these needed her, and every one of them waited for her. That's not a character flaw. It's what happens when a company outgrows one person's ability to see everything and nobody has drawn the lines yet. I built a two-minute check so you can see your own list. Link on the last slide."

## 8. Reply bank (for Shannon, when results come in)

Short replies she can send to people who hit reply. Edit freely.

- To an on-track result: "Nice. What did you do to get there? I'm collecting those stories."
- To a climbing result: "Every owner I've worked with started there. What's the one decision you're sick of making?"
- To a drifting result: "That's the one most owners never see. What was the biggest call made without you last month?"
- To a team member: "Send it to your owner. Compare trees Thursday. Tell me what you find."
- To "a question made me wince": "Which one? That's the one I want to hear about."
