**Section: Why this matters**

**H2:** You used to know everything. Now you decide everything.

Those aren't the same thing. When a company grows past one person, the decisions keep climbing to the top because nobody drew the lines. Not every decision carries the same weight, and your people can't tell which ones are theirs until somebody says so.

The Decision Tree is a plain way to draw those lines. This check shows you where the weight sits right now.

Owners and leaders of companies with roughly 20 to 200 people. Trades, manufacturing, family business, services. If you built it by being good at the work and now the work keeps coming back to you, this is for you.
